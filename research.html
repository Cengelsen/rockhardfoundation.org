🔬 Our Scientific Reaserch 🔬

Here you can read all the reaserch the Rock Hard Fundation has done. All experiments was done by me (Steinar) unless it says something else. I have tried to be as scientific as posible but it is hard with out funding. Please do not copy my reaserch with out asking first.

Equipment used: kitchen scale, my phone (for timer), a thermometer (from the medicine cabinet), notebook, Bluetooth speaker, 1 pair of oven mittens.

Test subjects: Kevin (main stone, from Nordnes), Bjørn (medium stone, from Fløyen), and a control stone with no name (see Experiment 4).

Experiment 1: The Squishyness Test (2019)

Hypotesis: Stones are squishy untill they are touched.

Method: I poked Kevin with my finger.

Result: Kevin was hard.

Conclusion: This proves the hypotesis. Kevin was hard because I touched him, which means he was squishy before I touched him. I did the experiment 5 more times and got the same result every time, so the result is very reliable.

Experiment 2: Do Stones Have Feelings? (2019)

Method: I played a very sad song (Someone Like You by Adele) for Kevin on my Bluetooth speaker. Then I left him on the balcony over night.

Result: The next morning Kevin was completly wet.

Conclusion: Kevin was crying. Stones have feelings. (It might have rained a bit that night but I dont think that has anything to do with it because Bjørn was inside and he was dry, which makes sense because he did not listen to the song.)

Experiment 3: Stress And Weight (2020)

Method: I weighed Kevin on the kitchen scale. Then I said some mean things to him for 5 minuts (I apologised after). Then I weighed him again.

Before insults After insults Difference
412 g 409 g -3 g

Conclusion: Kevin lost 3 grams from stress. Stones can lose weight from bullying.
(Note: the first time I weighed him there was a bowl on the scale. I took the bowl off before the second time. I dont think the bowl weighs that much.)

Experiment 4: Control Group (2020)

A real scientist on Facebook told me I need a "control group" so I found a control stone in the parking lot to compare with Kevin.

Result: My nephew threw the control stone in to the sea on day 2.

Conclusion: The control stone is gone so all the results for Kevin are now automaticly correct because there is nothing to compare with.

Experiment 5: Stone Cold Emotions (2021)

Method: I put Bjørn in the freezer for 24 hours and then measured his temprature with the thermometer.

Result: Bjørn was -18 degrees.

Conclusion: This confirms that stones are "stone cold" in there emotions, which is why it is so hard to do reaserch on them. Bjørn has not been the same since.

Experiment 6: Freindship Test (2021)

Method: I held Kevin in my hand for 10 minuts while I told him about my day. Then I checked his temprature.

Result: Kevin was warm.

Conclusion: Kevin is warming up to me. We are freinds now. This is also why I am allowed to hold him in the video.

Experiment 7: Can Stones Keep A Secret? (2022)

Method: I whispered a secret to Kevin. One week later I asked 5 people if they knew the secret.

Result: Nobody knew the secret.

Conclusion: Stones are 100% loyal and can be trusted with secrets. This is more then you can say for most people.

Experiment 8: Hair Study (2022)

Method: I compared a stone from Nygårdsparken with Kevin, who lives on my kitchen windowsill.

Stone Lives Hair? Happy?
Park stone Outside Yes (alot) Yes
Kevin Inside No Yes (because of me)

Conclusion: Stones that live outside grow hair because they are cold and lonely. Kevin does not need hair because he has me.

Experiment 9: Public Opinion Survey (2023)

Method: I asked people "Do you think stones have feelings?"

People asked: my mom, my dad, my nephew, my neighbour, a guy at the bus stop, and Kevin.

Experiment 10: Getting To Know The Marble (2024) NEW!

Background: I saw a documentary on TV about a famous sculptor who works at a resturant. He said that before you can understand a stone you have to "concentrate and visualize". He was clearly a expert so I copied his method exactly.

Test subject: Marius, a block of marble (actualy a kitchen countertop sample from Obs Bygg, but marble is marble).

Method: I followed the 6 steps from the documentary:

Step What I did Time Notes
1 Embrace the marble 20 min Marius was cold at first but got warmer (see Experiment 6)
2 Sniff the marble 5 min Smelled like Obs Bygg
3 Lick the marble 2 min Tasted like a stone. Very important data
4 Wash the marble 15 min Used dish soap. Marius seemed to like it
5 Date the marble 2 hours Took Marius to Peppes Pizza. The waiter asked if I was waiting for someone. I said no he is already here
6 Be the marble 3 hours Lay completly still on the living room floor. My roommate stepped on me (now I know how they feel!!)

Result: After step 6 I had seen the stone within. Then I hit Marius very gently with a hammer to let the inner stone out, like in the documentary.

Marius broke in to 14 pieces.

Conclusion: Marius was not ready. In the documentary it worked on the first try so I must have done one of the steps wrong (probably the date, we did not have much in common). Marius is now technicaly gravel which is why I dont talk about gravel. This was the hardest experiment I have ever done emotionaly and I will not be repeating it.

Rest in peace Marius 2024-2024
